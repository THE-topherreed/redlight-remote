Install RedLight Firmware

This installer only works in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer (not a phone or tablet) - it uses your browser's USB connection to talk to the board.
Step 1 Connect your CYD touchscreen board to this computer with a USB cable.
Step 2 Click the button below, then choose the board's port when your browser asks.
Step 3 Click "Install" and wait for the progress bar to finish (about a minute).
Sorry, your browser doesn't support this. Please use Chrome or Edge on a desktop/laptop computer. This page must be served over HTTPS to install firmware.

Once installed, the board will power on and show a "Connect to WiFi: RedLight-Setup" screen. Follow the printed setup guide from there.
