Setup Guide

RedLight

A touchscreen Record / Play / Stop control for your vocal booth, wired to your DAW over WiFi — no computer inside the booth required. Setup takes about ten minutes.

01

What's in the box

One touchscreen remote board and a USB cable. That's it — everything else runs on your existing studio computer and home WiFi.

You'll also need: a 2.4GHz WiFi network (see note below) and a USB power source near where you'll mount the board — a wall adapter, or a spare port on your interface or computer.

2.4GHz WiFi only. The board's radio doesn't support 5GHz networks. If your home WiFi name is the same on both bands, check your router for a separate 2.4GHz network name — most routers offer one.
02

Install the desktop app

On the computer running your DAW, install RedLightInstaller.exe. It's a small app that lives quietly in your system tray and translates taps on the touchscreen into keystrokes inside your DAW.

Download RedLightInstaller.exe →

Windows will likely show a "Windows protected your PC" screen since this app isn't yet signed with a paid certificate — click More info, then Run anyway. This is normal for small, independently-made apps.

The first time it runs, a Settings page opens automatically in your browser — no need to find anything. You'll see a 6-digit Pairing Code, which is how the touchscreen finds and authenticates with this specific computer.

To get back to Settings later, look for a small red dot icon near the clock in your taskbar — click the ^ arrow next to the clock first if you don't see it right away, since Windows sometimes tucks new icons away there.

Pairing Code 4 8 1 2 0 6

Keep this window open — you'll type this code into the touchscreen in Step 4.

04

Connect it to your WiFi

The first time it powers on, the board creates its own temporary WiFi network so it can ask you for your home network's details — no separate app or computer needed for this part.

On your phone or laptop: connect to the WiFi network named RedLight-Setup. A setup page should open automatically (if it doesn't, open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1).

On that page, choose your home WiFi network, enter its password, and paste in the Pairing Code from Step 2. Tap Save — the board will restart and connect.

The board finds your computer automatically on the network once paired — there's no IP address to type in, and it'll keep finding it even if your router hands out a new address later.
05

Match your DAW's shortcuts

Back in the desktop app's settings page, tell it your DAW's window name and its keyboard shortcuts for Record, Play, and Stop. Most DAWs list these under a Keyboard Shortcuts or Key Commands menu.

Click Test next to each field with your DAW open to confirm it fires correctly before you leave the booth.

Mount it and record

Fix the board somewhere visible and reachable in the booth — a mic stand clamp, a shelf, or adhesive mount all work. Keep the desktop app running (it starts automatically each time the studio computer turns on) and you're set: tap RECORD before you sing, STOP when you're done, PLAY to hear it back.

Troubleshooting

Screen says "Disconnected." Confirm you're on a 2.4GHz network and that your router is powered on. If you recently changed WiFi passwords, hold your finger anywhere on the screen for 5 seconds while powering the board on — this clears the saved network and re-opens the RedLight-Setup pairing flow from Step 4.

Buttons don't do anything in the DAW. Make sure the desktop app is running (check the system tray) and that the DAW's window title in Settings actually matches part of its real window title. Use the Test buttons in Settings to isolate the problem.

Play and Stop do the same thing. Some DAWs bind both to one toggle key. Check your DAW's key command list for separate dedicated Play/Stop shortcuts rather than a single transport-toggle key.
